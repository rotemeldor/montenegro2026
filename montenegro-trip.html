✈ Arrive
⛅ — Aug 21 · Žabljak
En route
Breakfast stop — Manitovac Restaurant, Nikšić
5 min from Nikšić centre, right on the river Zeta. Huge outdoor meadow, petting animals & play area for kids. Rated 4.6 on Google, 720 reviews. Open from 8AM. Budget ~45–60 min. After breakfast, fuel all 5 cars at the Petrol station on Bulevar 13. Jula on the way out of town — it's right on your route north toward Šavnik. Stick to Petrol or Lukoil, avoid EKO. Almost nothing between Nikšić and Žabljak after that. ⛽
